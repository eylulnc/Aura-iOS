# Privacy Policy — Aura

**Last updated:** July 10, 2026

Aura is a personal mood journal app. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights over it.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

### Account data (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, Aura receives:
- Your display name (if provided by Google or Apple)
- Your email address
- Your profile photo URL (Google only)

With Sign in with Apple, you may also choose to hide your email behind a private relay address — Aura works the same either way.

Sign-in is entirely optional. Aura works in full guest mode with no account required.

### Mood data
When you log a mood entry, Aura stores:
- The mood you selected
- An optional short note (if you add one)
- The date and time of the entry

### Notifications
If you enable daily reminders, Aura schedules local notifications on your device at the time you choose. No data about your notification settings or usage leaves your device.

### Home screen widgets
If you add the Aura widgets to your home screen, they read your locally stored mood data (today's mood, current streak) to display it. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere by the widget.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Stored

| Mode | Where data is stored |
|---|---|
| Guest (no sign-in) | On your device only, in a local database |
| Signed in | On your device and in your private Firebase Firestore document, accessible only to your account |

No mood data is ever shared with other users or made publicly accessible.

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features — displaying your mood history, calculating streaks, showing trends, and powering home screen widgets and reminder notifications. It is never:

- Sold to third parties
- Used for advertising
- Shared with anyone other than you
- Used to train AI or machine learning models

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Aura uses the following services:

- **Firebase Authentication** — handles Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple securely. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Cloud Firestore** — stores your mood entries when signed in. Data is stored in Google's cloud infrastructure.
- **Sign in with Apple** — handled entirely by Apple. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

No other third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are used.

---

## 5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data at any time:

- **Delete all data** — permanently removes all mood entries from your device and from the cloud; your account stays active (Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Data)
- **Delete account** — permanently removes your account and all associated data everywhere (Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account)
- **Sign out** — signs you out while keeping your local data intact
- **Disable reminders** — turn off daily notifications at any time (Settings → Reminders)
- **Remove widgets** — remove any Aura widget from your home screen at any time, like any other iOS widget

---

## 6. Data Retention

Your data is retained until you delete it. Deleting your account or all data removes it permanently and cannot be undone.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Aura is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
